# Innovation Project — BioGlow Season

> **Season prompt:** *"This season, your challenge is to identify a problem that puts biodiversity at risk and design an innovative solution that can help."*

**Rubric alignment is the point.** The judging rubric (see `00-judging-rubric.md`) scores the Innovation Project in five phases: **IDENTIFY → DESIGN → CREATE → ITERATE → COMMUNICATE**. Our process below is CJ's original 7 steps, tightened and re-numbered so every step lands inside a rubric phase — with one important addition at the end (Step 8: Presentation), because COMMUNICATE is 2 of the 10 scored rows and the original 7 steps never got the team ready for it.

---

## The Process

### Phase: IDENTIFY

**Step 1 — Research & Idea Making**
Explore biodiversity problems. Document *everything*: sources, articles, videos, field trips, interviews. The rubric explicitly rewards "clear, detailed research from a **variety of sources**" — so track source diversity, not just source count.

Track per idea: problem statement draft, why it matters, who it affects, sources consulted, which student contributed it.

**Step 2 — Idea Decision**
Lock in one problem via a structured decision (vote, decision matrix, or both — a simple scoring matrix of *impact / feasibility / team excitement / innovation potential* makes for great judging evidence). Record the runner-up ideas and *why* they lost; judges love hearing a real selection story.

Output: a **one-sentence problem definition**. The rubric's very first row is "clear definition of the problem" — write it, test it on an adult who wasn't in the room, refine it.

### Phase: DESIGN

**Step 3 — Expert Identification & Outreach**
Identify 1–3 experts (scientists, park rangers, university folks, industry). Track: who we contacted, who responded, meeting dates, what we asked, what we learned. *(Note: expert input pays off in both IDENTIFY — research variety — and ITERATE — feedback. Starting outreach early means the same expert can serve both.)*

**Step 4 — Project Planning**
Build the project plan: milestones, task owners, materials list, budget, deadlines working back from competition day. The rubric scores "clear evidence of an **effective project plan**" and "development process involved **all team members**" — so the plan should show every student's name on real tasks.

### Phase: CREATE

**Step 5 — Building the Solution**
Develop the innovative solution and build a **prototype model or drawing** (explicitly required by the rubric). Document the build with photos and dated log entries. Be ready to articulate *what is innovative* about it: is it new, or a new twist/application of something existing? Both are allowed — but the explanation is scored.

### Phase: ITERATE

**Step 6 — Testing, Sharing & Feedback**
Two distinct activities, both scored:
- **Test** the solution/prototype ourselves and record results.
- **Share** it with *multiple people/groups* (the rubric threshold for Accomplished) — experts from Step 3, other classes, parents, community groups. Log every share: audience, date, feedback received.

**Step 7 — Improve (loop back to 5 & 6)**
Turn feedback into concrete changes. Log each improvement as **feedback → change made → result**. This before/after trail is exactly what "clear evidence of improvements based on feedback" means.

### Phase: COMMUNICATE

**Step 8 — Presentation (suggested addition)**
Build and rehearse the 5-minute presentation: the problem, the research, the solution, its **potential impact on others**, and the team's journey. Every student speaks. Practice in front of an audience at least twice, and make it fun — "pride and enthusiasm" is a scored row. Celebrate the milestones along the way and show that celebration in the presentation.

## Step-to-Rubric Map (for the dashboard)

|---|---|---|
| 1. Research & Idea Making | IDENTIFY | Research variety ⚙️ |
| 2. Idea Decision | IDENTIFY | Problem definition |
| 3. Expert Outreach | IDENTIFY / ITERATE | Research variety ⚙️, Sharing |
| 4. Project Planning | DESIGN | Project plan, All members ⚙️ |
| 5. Building | CREATE | Innovation ⚙️, Model/drawing |
| 6. Testing & Feedback | ITERATE | Sharing, Improvements |
| 7. Iterate | ITERATE / CREATE | Improvements, Innovation ⚙️ |
| 8. Presentation | COMMUNICATE | Explanation & impact ⚙️, Pride ⚙️ |

## What the daily log should capture for this component

- New research sources (with type: article / video / interview / site visit)
- Ideas generated and who proposed them
- Decisions made and how (vote results, matrix scores)
- Expert contacts and conversations
- Prototype progress photos
- Every share-out: audience + feedback received
- Every improvement: feedback → change → result
- Each student's contribution that day

## Dashboard ideas for this component

- Phase progress bar (Identify → Communicate) with rubric-row status (1–4 self-assessment)
- Research source gallery grouped by source type
- Feedback → improvement pipeline (kanban: received / discussed / implemented)
- Expert contact log with next-touch reminders
- Countdown to presentation-rehearsal milestones
