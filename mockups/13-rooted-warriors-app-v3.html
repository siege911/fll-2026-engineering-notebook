The Problem

Divers and tide-pool visitors can't see bioluminescent algae blooms coming, so they miss them — or accidentally trample fragile glowing habitats at night.

Our Solution

GlowCast — a floating sensor buoy that measures water glow and texts a "bloom alert" with a safe-viewing map to the community.

How We Got Here

Started with 6 problem ideas → voted → researched 9 sources → interviewed 2 experts → built a cardboard buoy → got feedback → now building v2 with a real light sensor.
