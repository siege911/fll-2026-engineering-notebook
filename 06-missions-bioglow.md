# BIOGLOW™ Robot Game Missions (2026–27 season)

Reference copy of the official mission list, pulled from the **FLL Challenge BIOGLOW Robot Game Rulebook** (`../fll-challenge-bioglow-rgr.pdf`, pages 5 and 8–12). The rulebook wins if anything here disagrees with it.

**How to use this file**
- The app's Robot Game page uses these mission numbers, names, and max points.
- **"Our names" column:** whenever the team uses a nickname, a shorthand, or just a number instead of the official name ("Mission 2," "the rock one," "the seed shooter"), add it here so notebook entries, the app, and the rulebook can always be matched up. Keep adding aliases all season.
- Points marked *each* depend on how many pieces the mission model has. Piece counts were confirmed against the team's built models by CJ on 2026-09-06.

## Mission details

### M01 · Drone Survey (bags 1, 2) 🚫 no-equipment rule
High above the canopy, drones survey the landscape. Help the pilot launch the drone to create a LiDAR map of the rainforest.
- Drone is no longer touching the mat: **20**
- Bonus: and the LiDAR map is completely flipped over, with the scan marker at least partly in the survey area: **+10**
- Model parts: Drone, LiDAR Map, Scan Marker

### M02 · Exploding Seeds (bag 3)
Some trees disperse seeds by exploding their seed pods. Release the seeds from the pod.
- Seeds no longer touching the stalk: **10 each**
- Model parts: Stalk, Seeds (3)

### M03 · Flip the Rock (bags 4, 5)
Flip the rock to discover what's living underneath, but leave no trace by putting it back.
- The research flag is down: **20**
- Bonus: and the rock has been returned to its original starting position: **+10**
- Model parts: Rock, Research Flag

### M04 · Lucky Leaves (bag 6) 🚫 no-equipment rule
The katydid camouflages in the leaves. Collect leaves without disturbing it.
- One leaf completely removed, no longer touching the nest: **10**
- Bonus: and the second leaf is completely removed, with the katydid in its original starting position: **+20**
- Rules to remember: the nest is the model that holds the leaves and the katydid. If the katydid is completely removed from the nest *at any time*, the bonus is gone. If the katydid is even partly outside the leaf habitat (nest + marked mat area around it) at the end of the match, the mission scores **zero**.
- Model parts: Nest, Leaves (2), Katydid

### M05 · Reaching Roots (bag 7) 🚫 no-equipment rule
Help this plant's root cross the forest boundary in search of symbiosis.
- Plant root partially extended: **10** — OR — completely extended: **20** (only one scoring condition counts)
- Model parts: Plant Root
- Note: a fully extended plant root is also what gives the *other* team their M07 bonus.

### M06 · Leafcutter Frenzy (bags 8, 9, 10)
Guide the ants back to their nest, but move too fast and you scatter the leaves they collected.
- The ant is touching the nest, and the leaf fragments are contained within the nest: **10 each fragment**
- Model parts: Ant, Leaf Fragments (4 — confirmed on the built model), Nest

### M07 · Humongous Fungus (bag 10) 🚫 no-equipment rule
Form a new connection between the underground mycelium and a neighboring tree root.
- The mycelium is completely extended: **20**
- Bonus: and a connection is formed between one team's extended mycelium and the opposing team's fully extended plant root: **+10** (both teams can earn it; the pieces don't need to touch). Not possible at remote events or with no opposing team.
- Model parts: Mycelium

### M08 · Tangled (bags 11–15)
Climbing vines have wrapped around the tree. Untangle them.
- The vine is touching the mat: **30**
- Model parts: Vine (shares the big tree model with M09)

### M09 · Research Platform (bags 11–15) 🚫 no-equipment rule
Help Olivia and Michael deploy equipment to monitor the canopy.
- The research platform is raised: **10**
- The camera trap is deployed: **10**
- The seed is no longer touching the tree: **10**
- Model parts: Research Platform, Camera Trap, Seed (on the big tree model)

### M10 · Fragile Microhabitats (bag 16) 🚫 no-equipment rule
Move through the protected area without damaging tiny creatures' homes.
- The spider habitat is in its original starting position: **10**
- The snail habitat is in its original starting position: **10**
- Model parts: Spider Habitat, Snail Habitat
- These score by *not* being disturbed, so the robot's path matters.

### M11 · Window to the Past (bag 17)
Open the root window and document what you see.
- The root cover is down, touching the mat: **20**
- Model parts: Root Cover

### M12 · Forest Elder (bag 18) 🚫 no-equipment rule
An aging giant tree bends under its own weight. Add a support.
- The cane is completely raised, touching the tree: **20**
- The support tie is around the post: **10**
- Model parts: Cane, Support Tie, Post

### M13 · Keystone Species (bags 19, 20)
Build and deliver a keystone species to the newly protected habitat.
- Your team's keystone species is on the restoration platform, and the young trees are raised: **30**
- Rules to remember: build the keystone species from bag 20. It counts as *equipment*, is part of pre-match inspection, and the team must identify it then.
- Model parts: Restoration Platform, Young Trees, your Keystone Species

### M14 · Seeds of Renewal (bag 21)
Place the seeds your team collected in the soil.
- Seeds contained within the replantation station: **5 each**
- Bonus: and touching the mat: **+5 each**
- Model parts: Replantation Station, Seeds (3)

### M15 · Biocentric Architecture (bags 22, 23, 24) 🚫 no-equipment rule
Modify the building for wildlife homes, soil health, and natural light.
- The nesting canopy is raised: **10**
- The garden skylight is completely in: **10**
- The compost hatch is completely opened, touching the mat: **10**
- Environmental Bonus (only one possible): the model sits on one of three docks; completing that dock's greatest need earns **+10** — Mine dock → nesting canopy · City dock → garden skylight · Farm dock → compost hatch
- Model parts: Nesting Canopy, Garden Skylight, Compost Hatch

### Interchangeable Model Dock (Missions 13–15)
New this season: missions 13–15 use an interchangeable dock. The field setup decides which model goes on which dock, so choosing which one to attempt is part of mission strategy.

### Rules that appear on the mission pages
- **No Equipment Constraint symbol:** when it appears on a mission, that mission model cannot earn points if it is touching equipment at the end of the match. Applies only to the mission it's printed on.
- **Match:** 2.5 minutes (150 s), robot fully autonomous.
